Deep link landing

Tapping a link or scanning a QR opens the app directly, so this is the first thing a new person sees of Pamoja. The preview shows the group name, member count against the cap, and the weekly goal. It deliberately shows no member names and no avatars, because a forwarded link reaches strangers and real people's names should not be exposed to someone who has not joined. Name and count are enough to confirm you have the right group.

2B Join preview and loading — three facts, two buttons, nothing else. The avatar tile carries the group's own colour and initials, which is identity without exposing anybody. Loading keeps the exact same geometry so the resolve does not shift a single element.
