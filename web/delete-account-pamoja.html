Delete your Pamoja account (Arkayen Labs)

Last updated 20 August 2026

You can delete your Pamoja account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways, and both remove the same data.

Fastest way: delete inside the app

Immediate, and needs nothing from us.

  1. Open Pamoja and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Account actions.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Sign in again when asked. Nothing is deleted at this point.
  5. Confirm with Delete Permanently.

We ask you to sign in again first. That is a security requirement, not an obstacle: it stops someone who picks up an unlocked phone from destroying your account.

If you cannot open the app

Use this if you have uninstalled Pamoja, cannot sign in, or would simply rather we did it.

Email support@arkayenlabs.com with the subject “Delete my Pamoja account”.

Send it from the email address you signed in with, or tell us the phone number on the account, so we can identify it. We will never ask you for your password, and you should never send us one.

We action emailed requests within 30 days, and in practice far sooner, and confirm by reply once it is done. If we cannot match your request to an account we will tell you, rather than delete someone else’s data.

What is deleted

Permanently, with no recovery window:

DataWhat it is
Your profileName, and the age, height and weight you chose to enter
Your profile photoThe image file itself, removed from storage, not merely unlinked
Your step historyEvery daily step record Pamoja has stored for you
Your group membershipsYou leave every group, and each group’s member count and invite link are corrected
Your sign-in recordThe account itself, including the email address or phone number used to sign in

This cannot be undone. There is no recovery period and no archived copy to restore from. Signing up again later creates a genuinely new account with no history.

If you are the admin of a group

Groups are not destroyed when their admin leaves. Before your account is removed the group is handed to another member, so the people in it keep their group and their shared progress. If you are its only member, the group goes with you.

What is retained

A small amount of data outlives the account, none of which can identify you once it is gone:

  • The groups you belonged to. A group’s own record, its name and its combined weekly totals, belongs to that group rather than to you, so it survives your departure. Nothing in it identifies you once your membership is deleted.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics. Counts of events such as “a group was created”, never tied back to a person.
  • Crash reports, retained for up to 90 days so we can fix what caused them.
  • Records we are required by law to keep, where any apply.
  • Provider backups. Our infrastructure providers may hold fragments briefly, an unavoidable consequence of how backups work. They are never used to restore a deleted account and age out on their own.

Data on your device

Deleting your account clears what Pamoja stores on our side. Data held on the phone itself is separate:

  • Pamoja’s own settings and cache go when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its storage in Android Settings.
  • Your steps in Health Connect belong to Health Connect, not to us. Deleting your Pamoja account does not remove them, because they were never ours. Manage or delete them in the Health Connect app.

If you want Pamoja to stop reading your steps but want to keep your account, you do not need this page at all: revoke Pamoja’s access in Health Connect → App permissions. We stop reading new steps immediately.
