Group dashboard — the flagship

One screen that answers: are we going to make it, and where do I sit? The ring animates from empty on every open (900ms spring), the hero number counts up with it, and the small tick on the ring is the pace marker — where the week expects the group to be today. Being ahead of the tick is the feeling the product sells. ← Design system · Planned features →
