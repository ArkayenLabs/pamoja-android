Pamoja

Walk further, together

A shared-goal fitness app for small private circles. One combined weekly step target, pooled automatically from everyone's phone. No strangers, no shame, no scoreboard for the world — just the people who already care about you, pulling in the same direction.

Why this direction. Fitness UI defaults to cold neon-on-black — a gym at midnight. Pamoja is a kitchen table. So the identity is built on warm paper and ember: a clay-orange that reads as human effort and hearth rather than alarm, a jade that only appears when the group succeeds, and an ochre for the warming-up middle. The dark theme is espresso-tinted, never black — the same room with the lights low. Two interlocking rings are the mark, the progress ring, and the avatar shape all at once: individual circles that overlap into one. Type pairs a slightly quirky grotesque for headlines and hero numbers (personality, not corporate) with a humanist UI sans and a wide-tracked mono for labels, so data feels precise while voice stays casual.

Spacing, radii, elevation

Spacing · 4dp base

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40
Screen gutter 20dp. Card padding 18–20dp. Section gap 24dp. Bottom bar clears 96dp of content so nothing hides under it.

Radii

10 chip · 14 inner tile · 18 field · 22 card · 28 sheet · full pill for every button and stat pill. Nothing sharp anywhere — the shape language is round because the product is about circles of people.

Elevation strategy

Light: hairline → soft warm shadow (brown-tinted, never grey) → lifted sheet. Dark: shadows barely read, so depth comes from the four surface tiers plus a 4% warm top highlight on cards and a 10% hairline.

06

Motion & accessibility

Everything springs. Nothing eases linearly. The ring is the signature: it should feel like breath, not a loading bar.

Motion language

Ring fill — 900ms spring (stiffness 120, damping 14), ~2% overshoot then settle. Stroke sweeps from 12 o'clock while the hero number counts up in sync and the stage color cross-fades at the 40% and 100% thresholds.

Press feedback — scale 0.97 in 110ms, spring back over 240ms, plus an 8% ink overlay. Cards and list rows use the same scale so the whole UI feels like one material.

Screen transitions — shared x-axis: incoming slides 24dp and fades in over 300ms, outgoing drifts −16dp over 240ms. Back gesture reverses it. Group card → dashboard morphs the group tile into the top bar avatar.

List entrance — 12dp rise + fade, 280ms, staggered 40ms per row, capped at 8 rows.

Celebration — ring pulses three times, then breaks into concentric arc bursts (1400ms); hero number scales 1 → 1.08 → 1; haptic double-tap on completion.

Streak / badge unlock — badge pops 0.72 → 1.06 → 1 over 520ms with a glow ring expanding and fading behind it.

Reduced motion — all of the above collapse to 120ms opacity fades; the ring appears already filled, counters render final values, confetti becomes a single static arc burst.

Accessibility commitments

Contrast — every text pair listed in §01/§02 meets AA. Ember-on-white is used at 16px+ weight 600 only; ember text on the ember tint is replaced by ink.

Targets — 48 × 48dp minimum on every control, including the cheer button on leaderboard rows and the copy icon in Settings. Buttons are 56dp tall.

Never color alone — ring stages carry an icon + label; medal ranks carry the numeral; goal-hit rows carry a check; offline carries a cloud-off icon and a text banner.

Large font sizes — every row is a flex layout with wrapping text and no fixed heights below content; hero numbers shrink one step at 130% and the stat pills stack at 160%.

One-handed — primary actions live in the bottom third: floating action bar on Home, sticky primary button on all forms, cheer actions on the row itself. Top bar holds only back, title and share.

Screen readers — the ring announces "62 percent of weekly goal, 26,880 steps remaining, 3 days left"; leaderboard rows announce rank, name, weekly total.
